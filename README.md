# Awesome-Consumer-Goods-CRM

## Top Consumer Goods CRM Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  

*Focused on Retail Execution, Field Sales & Trade Promotion Management*  

**Last updated: March 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Consumer Goods CRM**. These tools help CPG manufacturers, distributors, and field teams manage retail audits, van sales, order capture, trade promotions, and direct store delivery (DSD) operations.



**Examples** include Salesforce Consumer Goods Cloud, AFS Technologies, Repsly, StayinFront, Pitcher, Promax, XTEL, Kantar XTEL, UpClear, and RW3 Technologies (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with every major active project for self-hosting, custom field sales workflows, and open retail execution platforms — ideal for CPG brands, distributors, and developers seeking transparent, vendor-independent consumer goods CRM.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms



- **[Salesforce Consumer Goods Cloud](https://www.salesforce.com/)**  

  Purpose-built CRM for CPG manufacturers with retail execution, van sales, trade promotion management, and direct store delivery. Features Einstein AI for outlet visit prioritization, order recommendations, and perfect store scoring.



- **[AFS Technologies](https://www.afstech.com/)**  

  ERP and CRM platform for food and beverage, consumer goods, and distribution. Features order management, trade promotion, warehouse management, and DSD routing.



- **[Repsly](https://www.repsly.com/)**  

  Retail execution platform for field teams. Features store audits, shelf compliance checks, order capture, photo verification, and GPS tracking.



- **[StayinFront](https://www.stayinfront.com/)**  

  Retail execution and CRM platform for consumer goods. Features mobile field sales, merchandising, trade promotion, and analytics.



- **[Pitcher](https://www.pitcher.com/)**  

  Sales enablement and retail execution platform for CPG. Features guided selling, order capture, CRM sync, and closed-loop marketing.



- **[Promax](https://www.promax.com/)**  

  Field sales and merchandising platform for CPG brands. Features retail audits, planogram compliance, and sales reporting.



- **[XTEL](https://www.xtel.com/)**  

  Trade promotion management and revenue growth management platform for CPG. Features promotion planning, ROI analysis, and deductions management.



- **[Kantar XTEL](https://www.kantar.com/)**  

  Combined offering integrating Kantar's consumer panel data with XTEL's TPM platform for data-driven trade promotion decisions.



- **[UpClear](https://upclear.com/)**  

  Trade promotion management and revenue optimization platform. Features promotion planning, accrual management, and analytics for CPG brands.



- **[RW3 Technologies](https://www.rw3.com/)**  

  Retail execution and DSD platform for food and beverage. Features route accounting, van sales, and direct store delivery management.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[ERPNext](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext)**  

  Comprehensive open-source ERP (GPL-3.0) with integrated CRM, sales, inventory, and distribution modules. Features customer management, quotation, order capture, and route planning via field sales extensions. 30k+ GitHub stars. The most complete open-source alternative for consumer goods distribution and retail execution .



- **[Odoo](https://github.com/odoo/odoo)**  

  Open-source business suite (LGPL-3.0) with CRM, Sales, Inventory, and Point of Sale modules. Features customer management, sales orders, van sales extensions, and trade promotion via community modules. 40k+ GitHub stars. Enterprise version adds advanced field service and route planning.



- **[Dolibarr](https://github.com/Dolibarr/dolibarr)**  

  Open-source ERP/CRM (GPL-3.0) with customer relationship management, order management, stock management, and point of sale. Suitable for small to mid-sized CPG distributors and wholesalers.



- **[Vtiger CRM](https://github.com/vtiger-crm/vtigercrm)**  

  Open-source CRM (MPL-2.0) with sales force automation, customer portal, and inventory management. Features quotes, orders, and basic field sales tracking.



- **[SuiteCRM](https://github.com/salesagility/SuiteCRM)**  

  Open-source CRM (AGPL-3.0) with accounts, contacts, opportunities, and quotes. Extensible via modules for retail execution and field sales workflows.



- **[EspoCRM](https://github.com/espocrm/espocrm)**  

  Lightweight open-source CRM (GPL-3.0) with accounts, contacts, leads, opportunities, and custom entities. REST API supports integration with mobile field apps and inventory systems.



- **[Krayin CRM](https://github.com/krayin/laravel-crm)**  

  Laravel-based open-source CRM (MIT) with leads, contacts, and sales pipelines. Extensible for consumer goods workflows including outlet management and order capture.



- **[OpenBoxes](https://github.com/openboxes/openboxes)**  

  Open-source supply chain and inventory management system (EPL-1.0). Features purchase orders, stock management, and shipment tracking. Used by NGOs and distributors for CPG logistics.



- **[Medusa](https://github.com/medusajs/medusa)**  

  Open-source headless commerce platform (MIT) with product catalog, pricing, and order management. Suitable for building custom B2B ordering portals for retailers and distributors.



- **[Saleor](https://github.com/saleor/saleor)**  

  Open-source GraphQL-first e-commerce platform (BSD-3-Clause) with multi-channel support. Can power B2B ordering and trade promotion portals for CPG brands.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **[Akeneo PIM](https://github.com/akeneo/pim-community-dev)** — Open-source Product Information Management (OSL-3.0) for managing complex CPG product catalogs.

- **[Pimcore](https://github.com/pimcore/pimcore)** — Open-source PIM/MDM platform (GPL-3.0) with product data and digital asset management.

- **[Grocy](https://github.com/grocy/grocy)** — Open-source household/grocery management (MIT) adaptable for small retail inventory.

- **[Snipe-IT](https://github.com/snipe/snipe-it)** — Open-source asset management (AGPL-3.0) for tracking field equipment and promotional assets.

- **[Open Source POS](https://github.com/opensourcepos/opensourcepos)** — Open-source point of sale (MIT) for retail transactions.

- Community **n8n** workflows for retail audit automation and Slack alerts.

- **Field Sales mobile apps** built with React Native or Flutter connecting to open CRMs via REST API.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Combine **ERPNext** or **Odoo** as the ERP/CRM backend with **Medusa** or **Saleor** for B2B ordering portals, **Akeneo PIM** for product data management, and **Open Source POS** for retail transactions — creating a fully self-hosted, enterprise-grade consumer goods CRM stack.
